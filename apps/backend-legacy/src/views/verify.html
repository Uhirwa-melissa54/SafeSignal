Welcome to SafeSignal

SafeSignal blocks harmful websites such as adult content, gambling, violence, malware and phishing.

Your parent wants to enable this protection on your device.

Installation Instructions

Please follow these steps to enable SafeSignal on your Android device:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Go to Network & Internet or Connections
  3. Select Private DNS
  4. Choose Private DNS provider hostname
  5. Enter: dns.safesignal.local
  6. Save changes.
